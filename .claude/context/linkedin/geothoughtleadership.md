Most SEO is backward-looking. The best SEO is predictive.

Legacy SEO is largely reactive. You look at what people are already searching for, check the volume, assess the competition, and create content to match that demand.

That worked well previously, but it's not where the outsized returns come from. The real leverage comes from anticipating macro shifts before the volume fully materializes, because by the time search volume shows up clearly in the tools, the window is already closing.

I learned this lesson while building my last company, when streaming services started emerging as alternatives to cable TV. Terms like "how to watch ESPN without cable" or "stream HBO without a subscription" had low search volume, but I saw where the behavior was headed. We created content for those queries before the demand spiked, and when the surge came, we were already properly positioned.

Anticipating search volume is really about anticipating behavior. What is changing in the industry? What new workflows are emerging? What questions will people inevitably ask once this shift compounds?

Good SEO matches demand while great SEO forecasts it.

The difference between reactive and anticipatory SEO isn't marginal. It compounds over time, and by the time it's obvious in the data, it's usually too late to catch up.
